Example Same moves, different face

The face changed. The performance didn't.

The original creator (left in each pair) and a suspected copy (right). We map a full-body skeleton on every frame and compare posture: where the arms, hands and head are, moment by moment. Even with a different face, the copy repeats the original's movements beat for beat.

Faces and personal details are blurred for privacy. Timestamps show where each moment appears in the original (A) and the suspected copy (B).

Method

Seven models, three tests. If any test matches, it was copied.

Commercial deepfakes swap whatever they need to (the face, the voice, the script or the background) and keep the rest. So we test each part separately and report the strongest match. We sample two frames per second from each video and transcribe its speech. Only moments where a person is clearly on screen (the person detector is confident and the person cut-out is large enough) are compared visually, so title cards, black frames and empty shots can't create false matches.

  1. Pose estimation Posture: which way the arms, hands, head and body point, and where the hands and head sit relative to the shoulders. Matches the same moves even when re-performed by someone else, cropped or zoomed. Body & motion
  2. Body-part segmentation Maps the head, hair, upper body, arms, hands and lower body, and compares how those regions are laid out in the frame. Body & motion
  3. Surface normals The direction each patch of skin and fabric faces: fine 3D surface detail. Body & motion
  4. Pointmap Relative depth of every pixel of the person: the overall 3D body shape. Body & motion
  5. Human matting The exact silhouette, including hair and loose clothing. Also used to cut the person out for the scene test. Body & motion
  6. Image fingerprints Compares the background square by square (table, wall, ceiling, props), ignoring the person. Catches the same room, set and shot with someone else edited in. Scene & visuals
  7. Speech to text Transcribes what's said; the two scripts are then compared phrase by phrase. Catches a reused script read by a new face or AI voice. Script

Each test looks for matched sections rather than one start-to-finish match, so a copy that is trimmed, re-ordered, sped up, mirrored, or only uses a few seconds of the original inside a longer video still lines up, and each section is listed with its timestamps. Each test is calibrated on a labelled set of known copies and unrelated videos, and the final score is the highest of the three.

Privacy notice

Videos you upload, and the analysis results, are stored privately so we can run the comparison, show you the result, and review and improve the system. They are not public and are not sold or shared. Your result is visible to anyone you give its link to. When you sign in with Google we keep your email address to run your account and usage limits. If you join the creator waitlist, we keep the email and channels you give us to contact you about scanning your content. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your card details. We also store a one-way hash of your network address to prevent abuse, not the address itself.

Only upload videos you have the right to use. The service compares footage; it does not identify who a person is. To have your uploads deleted, email sj@creatorgen.ai with your result link.
